# Editorial checklist

Use this checklist after running the deterministic audit script.

## Scope and claim quality

- The brief names one concrete audience and one release decision.
- Every quantitative claim has a visible basis or is labeled as an estimate.
- The brief separates observed results from planned work.

## Technical traceability

- The reader can tell which artifact, route, or model produced each result.
- Token counts and context-budget claims use an explicit source.
- Failure states are preserved instead of being rewritten as successes.

## Delivery readiness

- The brief states how the result will be verified after release.
- High-risk changes have a rollback path.
- The next owner can act without reading the entire project history.
